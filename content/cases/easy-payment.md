## The problem

Around 2022, many Korean consumer brands launched their own easy pay. CGV, a cinema chain we processed payments for, wanted one too.

Paying by card in its app took four steps, two of them outside the app: choose a card company, open its app, authenticate, come back.

![Card checkout before: four steps, two of them outside the app. After easy pay: choose a saved card, enter a 6-digit PIN, paid.](images/easy-pay/checkout-steps.svg)

## My role

Team member, working with a senior colleague. The merchant built the app; our team delivered the payment integration behind it.

- **Card companies:** set up dedicated merchant IDs with each of the eight major Korean card companies
- **Processing and authentication:** terminal IDs through the card-processing company; authentication flow confirmed with the provider
- **Reference data, integration and testing** with the merchant's business team and engineering
- **After launch:** monitored payments and handled issues

## Result

Customers register a card once, then pay with a 6-digit PIN without leaving the app. Card-issuer promotions ran on it too.

The setup was reused for CJ The Market, the group's online food store (pilot from February 2023), and became the base of CJ PAY, launched in February 2024.
