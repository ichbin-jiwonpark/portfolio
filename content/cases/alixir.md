## Design question

How can we design intuitive and engaging digital platform features that support Gen Z in discovering, sharing and adopting healthier and culturally mindful cooking habits?

## Ideation

We mapped utopian and dystopian futures from 2025 to 2050 on a futures cone. Two threads came together: on the utopian side, personalised and culturally rooted nourishment; on the dystopian side, the environmental and social cost of trend-driven food, where ingredients bought for viral recipes put pressure on producers and natural resources.

The concept that connected them: **a smart food device that turns everyday fruit and vegetables into vibrant, exotic creations from around the world.**

## Concept

The scenario has three steps:

1. **Pick your produce:** bring everyday fruit and vegetables from the supermarket, or use the machine's built-in ingredients
2. **Choose your exotic creation:** for example a dragon fruit or a starfruit
3. **Transform and enjoy:** the device recreates the chosen food's shape, colour, texture and taste from the ingredients

The benefits we set out include less food waste, lower shipping emissions, less demand for crops tied to exploitative labour, and exotic produce without imports. The brand name combines "alle" (German for "all"), "exotic" and "elixir", with the line "Transform Everyday into Extraordinary".

## Landing page

From a moodboard, we set the direction for the page: a clean, structured grid, trendy but readable type, fresh mint green, neon yellow and purple, playful touch effects such as bouncing fruit, and gacha-like transitions between choosing an ingredient and seeing the result.
