## Background

## Ideate

The concept comes from poomsae, the forms in Taekwondo, where each movement flows into the next. Its line is "The flow never breaks."

- **Experience points never decrease.** A missed day does not take anything away.
- **Rest is recorded too** [what +3 / +2 / +1 stand for].
- **A belt system** shows long-term progress [how belts are earned].
