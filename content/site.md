I'm Jiwon Park. I work on products where rules, money and people meet.

Before moving to Germany, I worked in the payment business of a large South Korean group, running payment services for its cinema, retail and streaming brands. I looked after more than 20 payment methods, added a second processing route for live card payments, and led the company's first anti-money-laundering system, from learning the regulation to designing the screens staff use every day.

In 2026 I completed an MA in Visual & Experience Design at the University of Europe for Applied Sciences in Potsdam. My thesis, POOM, is a wellbeing app concept that treats rest as progress.

I live in Berlin. I work in English and Korean, and I'm learning German (B1).
